## Trial Reels: how to use them
- Trial Reels are shown to non-followers first. Use them for new angles, riskier opinions and new formats, never for your best proof.
- Every Trial is a new video. Never post a grid Reel again as a Trial; re-uploads are what got suppressed.
- After 72 hours: if it beats ~900 views (your median) or gets 5+ shares or saves, share it to your profile. If not, keep it as a test result and move on.
