# Ronit Ranks: celebrity brand campaigns (script)

Length: ~75–85s. Ronit to camera. Each brand gets a logo or ad still with the rank number on screen, about 6s per entry.
Cover: CELEB ADS: RANKED / "#10 will make Ranveer angry"

HOOK (0–4s)
Ronit: "Ranveer Singh just launched a brand with boAt's founder. I ranked it dead last. Below an ad that's literally in court."
On screen: RANVEER'S BRAND = #10 😬

SETUP (4–8s)
"10 celebrity brand campaigns. Ranked by brand value, not star power. Let's go."

#10, H.I.M. × Ranveer Singh + Aman Gupta
"H.I.M. Handsome Indian Man. Ranveer plus Aman Gupta, launched last week. And right now, it's a celebrity and a slogan. Men's grooming is one of the most crowded shelves in India. Why should I switch? I haven't been told yet. Fame gets you the launch. It doesn't get you the second purchase. Ask me again in six months."

#9, Vimal Elaichi × Ajay Devgn
"Crores on a superstar, and the ad is now being challenged in the Bombay High Court. When the ad needs a lawyer, the brand has a problem."

#8, Rangeela Vodka × Ranveer Singh
"Yes, Ranveer again. Two brands, both bottom three. The energy is unmatched, but tell me one thing this bottle stands for besides Ranveer."

#7, SEVEN × MS Dhoni
"Dhoni is the brand. SEVEN isn't. Name one SEVEN product. Exactly."

#6, Mokobara × Diljit Dosanjh
"Half the internet called it a copy. The other half called it genius. That's attention. It's not ownership."

#5, Zomato × Saif Ali Khan
"Bringing back the 'WOW' meme? Fun. But Zomato does a meme every festival. Fun isn't new."

#4, ChatGPT × Sachin Tendulkar
"Even AI needs a human face in India. And no face says 'trust me' like Sachin."

#3, Aspora × Yuvraj Singh
"Yuvi for trust, a thousand creators for reach. That's not an ad, that's a sales team. Brutal comments, but a smart system."

#2, Honey Singh × his tequila
"He didn't launch a tequila ad. He launched a song. And nobody skips a Honey Singh song."

#1, Airbnb × Raichand House
"Kareena. Hrithik. Karan Johar. And 25 years of K3G. Airbnb didn't make an ad. They let you walk into your childhood. That's brand value."

CLOSE (last 5s)
"Celebrities get you attention. Only a story gets you a brand. Ranveer fans, the comments are open. Which one did I get wrong?"
On screen: WHICH ONE DID I GET WRONG? 👇

## Notes
- #9: say "being challenged in court", never "illegal". The case is ongoing.
- #10 is an opinion about a brand that's a week old. Keep "ask me again in six months" in; it makes the take fair and the rage bait safe.
- Plan Part 2: reply to the angriest Ranveer-fan comments.
