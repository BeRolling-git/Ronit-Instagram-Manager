# Ronit Ranks: celebrity brand campaigns (script v2, rage-bait)

Length: ~75–85s. Ronit to camera, deadpan. Logo or ad still plus the rank number on screen, about 6s per entry.
Cover: CELEB ADS: RANKED / "Ranveer, wahi purana style 😬"

HOOK (0–4s)
"Ranveer Singh launched another brand. I ranked it dead last. Below an ad that's literally in court."
On screen: RANVEER = #10 😬

SETUP (4–7s)
"10 celebrity brands. Ranked on brand value, not star power. Fans, sorry in advance."

#10, H.I.M. × Ranveer Singh + Aman Gupta
"Pehle SuperYou. Phir Rangeela. Ab H.I.M. Same script every time: Ranveer's face, Ranveer's energy, launch party, done. Wahi purana style, nothing new. Handsome Indian Man? Bhai, men's grooming already has fifty brands. Tell me why I should switch. Fame gets you the first order. It doesn't get you the second."

#9, Vimal Elaichi × Ajay Devgn
"Crores on a superstar, and the ad is now being challenged in court. When your ad needs a lawyer, your brand needs a rethink."

#8, Rangeela Vodka × Ranveer Singh
"Haan, Ranveer again. Same formula, different bottle. Take his face off the label. What's left?"

#7, SEVEN × MS Dhoni
"Dhoni is the brand. SEVEN is just the sticker. Name one SEVEN product. Exactly."

#6, Mokobara × Diljit Dosanjh
"Half the internet said copy. The other half said genius. Diljit got the attention. Mokobara just rented it."

#5, Zomato × Saif Ali Khan
"'WOW' again? Zomato's festive strategy: find last year's meme, add a celebrity, repeat. Funny? Yes. New? No."

#4, ChatGPT × Sachin Tendulkar
"An AI company needed Sachin to make Indians trust it. Think about that. Even AI can't beat the God of Cricket."

#3, Aspora × Yuvraj Singh
"Yuvi for trust, a thousand creators for reach. The comments roasted them, but that's a sales machine, not an ad."

#2, Honey Singh × his tequila
"Everyone else made an ad. Honey Singh made a song. Nobody skips a Honey Singh song, and that's the whole strategy."

#1, Airbnb × Raichand House
"Kareena. Hrithik. Karan Johar. 25 years of K3G. Airbnb didn't sell rooms. They sold your childhood back to you. That's not celebrity marketing. That's brand building."

CLOSE (last 5s)
"Celebrity gets you attention. Story gets you a brand. Ranveer fans, I'll wait in the comments."
On screen: RANVEER FANS, COMMENTS ARE OPEN 👇
