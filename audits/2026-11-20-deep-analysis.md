## Conclusions
1. Ronit's best format is ranking personal brands. It gets views (63k, 13.8k), it's built on his expertise (personal branding), and it's a recurring series people remember.
2. Named people and brands bring the reach. The answer isn't going back to ad decodes but decoding personal brands and founders: the people his ICP knows and wants to be like.
3. Pure founder/agency talk works as a once-a-week story with money and stakes, not as the main format.
4. Volume must come back: 1 solo Reel a day, never batches.
5. Views aren't turning into follows (+1 net in 30 days, 0 link taps). Recall needs named series, a fixed cover template and pinned flagship Reels.

## Recall system
- Series 1, "RONIT RANKS" (weekly): rank creators or founders by brand value, not followers. Same set, same opening ("I ranked…"), numbered tier-list cover.
- Series 2, "FOUNDER BRAND BREAKDOWN" (2x/week): one founder or creator's personal brand, what works and what doesn't.
- Series 3, "WHEN RONIT SAYS 'COME HERE'" (long form + 2 clips a week): agency life with money and stakes.
- Series 4, "CASE FILE" (every 2 weeks): client proof (Jigar, Dr. Prashant). This is the lead driver, with DM BRAND.
- One cover template per series (colour + series label in the corner). The same sign-off line every time.
- Pins: Beauty ranking (63k) · Fashion ranking (13.8k) · latest case study.
