# Audit: @ronitthakur18, reels under 1,000 views (2026-11-06)

Data: 104 Reels, Oct 12 – Nov 5, via the Meta API. 37 got 5–999 views; 27 got 0–4.
Not available: 3-second hold, watch time, non-follower share. This is based on view numbers, captions and Reel lengths only.

## The diagnosis in numbers
| | Reels with 1,000+ views (24) | Reels under 1,000 (19, excluding batch uploads) |
|---|---|---|
| Likes per view | 1.2% | 2.4% |
| Shares + saves per 1k reach | 2.4 | 4.4 |
| Median length | 50s | 37s |
| Reach above your 1,112 followers | all | 0 of 19 |

Read: the low Reels are liked *more* by the people who see them. Instagram never pushed them, because the first frame and line didn't stop people scrolling. It's a hook and packaging problem, not a weak idea.

## Batch uploads (Oct 15, 21, 26, Nov 5): 18 under 1,000 plus 27 near zero
Same diagnosis for all of them: lots of Reels uploaded together, many of them re-uploads. Captions are "Have you seen this ad?", an emoji line, or hashtags only.
Fix: delete or archive the duplicates. Post one Reel at a time, 17:30–20:30 IST, with a real hook.
Nov 5 re-uploads of "KBC" (0 views) and "Dating app marketing" (1 view) show re-uploading still gets suppressed.

## Rules for every Reel from now on
1. The hook names the viewer (founder, brand manager) or a specific person or brand in the first 2 seconds, with words on screen at frame 1.
2. One real detail per Reel: a number, a name, a ₹ amount. Quotes without a story are capped at about 300 views on this account.
3. One Reel at a time. No re-uploads.
4. One call to action that turns into a lead: DM "BRAND" (only used once so far).
5. Reply to comments in the first hour. 14 comments across 104 Reels means nobody is starting a conversation; end with a question your ICP has an opinion on.
